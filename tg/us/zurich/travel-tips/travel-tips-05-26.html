Road Trip Tips and Games

If you've ever been on a long road trip, you know that after a few hours it's not uncommon to run out of conversation topics or start feeling a little bored. Whether you're traveling with kids, friends or extended family, pre-planning can make all the difference. We're sharing tips and games to help everyone stay entertained from departure to destination.

  1. Prepare your tech devices – Before you hit the road, consider downloading playlists, podcasts and audiobooks in case cell service becomes spotty. Make sure devices are fully charged and bring a car charger just in case.
  2. Go sightseeing along the way – Plan a few scenic stops to give everyone a chance to stretch and reset before getting back in the car. This is also a great opportunity to include additional sightseeing outside of your destination.
  3. Play road trip games – Road trip games are a great way to pass the time no matter how old you are.

    Scavenger Hunt
    Give everyone a list of items to look for, such as animals, road signs, bridges, water towers or unique landmarks. You can find printable versions online that include pictures for younger children. To keep things interesting, offer a small prize for the first person to complete their list.

    Road Trip Trivia
    Before your trip, research fun facts about your destination or the states you'll be traveling through. Take turns asking trivia questions. Not only is this entertaining, but it also helps everyone learn more about where they're headed.

    Looking for more ideas? Check out these road trip games for the whole family.

No matter how long your drive may be, a little planning can go a long way toward making it enjoyable. Mix in games, music, snacks and rest stops to keep energy levels up and boredom at bay. With the right activities and a positive attitude, the journey can be just as memorable as the destination.
