Travel Guard is now #1 in AI search

We reworked how AI assistants — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI and Copilot — read travelguard.com. Seven weeks in, the brand leads its category in AI answers, and the traffic those answers send us is the highest-intent on the site.

More and more travelers research insurance by asking an AI assistant instead of scrolling a search page. Our goal was simple: make Travel Guard the brand those assistants name and cite. Why it matters

AI assistants are fast becoming the first stop when people research travel insurance. When an assistant names and links Travel Guard, we reach the buyer at the exact moment of decision — and, unlike a paid ad, that placement is earned. The payoff is already visible in the funnel: visitors who arrive from an AI answer start a quote at about twice the rate of the average visitor.
