Accordion · FAQ pattern

What does my Travel Guard plan cover?

Most plans include trip cancellation, medical expenses, baggage protection, and 24/7 emergency assistance. Specific limits vary by plan tier — see your Plan Document for full details.

When should I purchase a plan?

You can buy a plan up to the day before departure, but purchasing within 15 days of your initial trip deposit unlocks Pre-Existing Medical Conditions coverage on most plans.

How do I file a claim?

File online at travelguard.com/claims, or call us 24/7. Most claims are decided within 10 business days once we receive supporting documents.
