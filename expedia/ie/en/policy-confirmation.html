  • All insured persons must be 75 years & under to be eligible for the Cancellation Plan, and 65 years & under to be eligible for the Protection Plan.
  • Protection Plan provides cover of up to € 175,000 in total for both B1-Emergency Medical Expenses and B2 – Emergency Medical Evacuation & Repatriation if an insured person is diagnosed with COVID-19 during their travels.
Car rental excess products
  • All insured persons must be aged 18 or over and hold a valid driving licence entitling them to drive in the relevant country. This includes any additional drivers you add to your rental agreement.
  • This policy does not replace your motor insurance policy, nor is it a primary damage policy covering the rental vehicle itself. This policy only covers the amount you are responsible for under the terms of your car rental agreement up to the amount shown in the COI, and not the full value of the rental vehicle itself.
